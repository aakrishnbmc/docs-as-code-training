## Common problems

<details>
<summary>Cannot sign in</summary>

**Cause:** Usually a mistyped password or a lost network connection.

1. Re-enter your **username** and **password** (check that Caps Lock is off).
2. Confirm your device is connected to the internet.
3. Reset your password using the **Forgot password?** link if it still fails.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Application is not responding</summary>

**Cause:** Usually a temporary glitch or a weak connection.

1. Refresh the page, or close and reopen the application.
2. Check that your network connection is stable.
3. Restart your device if it remains unresponsive.

</details>

## Still need help?

If the problem persists, contact your system administrator.[^2] Include the error message, the steps you took, and the time it happened.

[^1]: You can find the version number under **Help > About** in <productname>.
[^2]: Your system administrator manages <productname> access and settings for your organization.
